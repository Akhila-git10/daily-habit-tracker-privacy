# Privacy Policy – Daily Habit Tracker

**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

Daily Habit Tracker ("the App") is developed by Akhila Narkenabilli.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Habit names, emojis, completion status, and habit history are stored locally on your device.

## 2. How Your Information Is Used

Your habit information is used only to provide the App's features, including habit tracking, daily progress, and habit history.

The current version does not actively send habit information to a developer-operated server.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your habit information with third parties.

The current version does not include advertising or analytics services.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your habit information is stored locally on your device. We use Android's local application storage.

Android backup is disabled for the App. We recommend keeping your own records if you want to preserve your information.

## 5. Data Deletion

You can delete individual habits within the App.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data removes the locally stored habit information.
